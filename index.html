I lead assigned language evaluation workflows, align annotators and QA on a consistent standard, and make the final quality call on work within my approval scope.

  1. AnnotatorReviews or labels the submission and records the evidence.
  2. QA reviewerChecks the judgment against the rubric and flags disagreements.
  3. Quality Assurance LeadResolves escalated calls, calibrates the team, and approves assigned work.
View responsibilities and working standards
  • Linguistic review

    Audit Filipino, Tagalog, and Taglish outputs for meaning, grammar, register, naturalness, terminology, and cultural fit.

  • Calibration

    Compare QA decisions against the rubric, document disagreements with examples, and align reviewers on approved interpretations.

  • Contributor feedback

    Explain each correction, reference the relevant criterion, and help annotators and QA resolve recurring errors.

  • Style guides and documentation

    Build consistent Tagalog evaluation guidelines, glossary entries, tone and register guidance, FAQs, and approved examples.

  • Onboarding and training

    Lead calibration discussions and walk contributors through sample judgments, common mistakes, and the expected evidence.

  • Coordination

    Share guideline clarifications and follow up on open issues with project leads, annotators, and QA reviewers.

  • Workflow support

    Surface blockers and support reviewer participation and quality priorities within assigned permissions.

  • AI data and model quality

    Distinguish language errors from factual, reasoning, safety, formatting, and instruction-following issues when the rubric calls for it.

  • Reporting and improvement

    Track recurring errors and rubric gaps. Recommend clearer instructions, examples, and escalation paths.

I check format, meaning, natural Filipino/Tagalog usage, rubric fit, and the evidence behind the judgment. Feedback gives a practical correction or next step.

    How I classify a finding

    Meaning and terminology

    Compare the rule, values, and glossary across screens. Flag an odds boundary that changes eligibility as a meaning defect.

    Text fit and layout

    Try concise wording first when meaning can be preserved. Report clipping, overlap, font, or layout problems that still need an interface fix.

    Keys, languages and placeholders

    Distinguish an untranslated string with a known key from a missing or untraceable key. Capture incorrect variables, empty values, and missing string-table messages.

    Function and configuration

    Record broken navigation or a workflow blocker separately from a string issue. Check mismatched icons, prompts, and scene references.

    Voice and regional fit

    Review natural register, punctuation, currency and date formats, cultural fit, and alignment between dubbing and subtitles. Use the project rubric to classify impact.

    Severity describes impact. Priority describes how soon the issue needs attention. I apply the active project’s reporting rules.
