# Review of supplied contact, section and navigation files

The references fit the established Sora/Manrope typography and black, gray, paper and red palette. Their chapter sequence, clear step validation, optional language/market context and reading-progress line are suitable additions.

They are standalone exports rather than drop-in modules for this repository:

- The contact scripts prepare mailto drafts; they do not deliver through FormSubmit. The final delivery step was replaced while retaining the current brief design and choices.
- Their global styles and reused IDs would compete with the current site if pasted wholesale. New styles and controls were scoped to the incumbent components.
- Their animation calls use a different `window.Assembly` interface. Integration uses the current portfolio events, motion preference, keyboard handling and root registration.
- The About excerpt points at another Site and an asset filename not used by this repository. Current internal workflow links and verified local CV assets were preserved.
- The navigation example's small-screen menu does not include a rule displaying `.nav-items` when `.open` is set. The incumbent accessible menu, Escape focus return and inert links were retained. The progress line was integrated without the reference's layout-based width animation.

The implementation keeps all eleven projects, sixteen complete roles and 75 toolkit entries. Existing biography, credentials, languages, contact options and professional metrics remain authoritative. New QA examples are expressly illustrative.
